Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about using Proftex and its earning activities.

What is Proftex? ＋

Proftex is an entertainment and digital-activity platform where users can explore different activities such as games, music, quizzes, chat activities, promotional spins and bonuses. The activities available to you may change over time.

How do I get started? ＋

Create your Proftex account using the official registration page. After registration, complete any required account setup and open your dashboard to see the activities currently available to you.

What are the ways to earn on Proftex? ＋

The current concept includes playing games, listening to music, approved chat activities, Spin & Win promotions, prediction content, weekly bonuses and quizzes. Additional activities can be added later.

Do I have to complete every activity? ＋

No. You can choose from the activities available to your account. Each activity can have its own requirements, limits and reward rules.

Can I use Proftex on my phone? ＋

Yes. The website is designed to work on smartphones, tablets and computers. A mobile-friendly interface makes it easy to check your activities while using your phone.

How do I earn from games? ＋

Open the games section, select an eligible game and follow the activity instructions. Rewards should only be credited when the required conditions are successfully completed.

How does paid music work? ＋

When music campaigns are available, you can select an eligible track or listening task and follow the stated requirements. The reward amount and eligibility should be displayed before you begin.

What does chatting involve? ＋

Chat activities are intended for friendly conversation through the approved Proftex system. Users should respect other participants, avoid sharing sensitive personal information and follow the platform's community rules.

How does Spin & Win work? ＋

Eligible users may receive access to promotional spins. A spin can produce different outcomes depending on the promotion. Any limits, eligibility requirements or terms should be shown clearly before the spin is used.

Are bet predictions guaranteed to win? ＋

No. Predictions are not guarantees. Sports and other betting outcomes are uncertain and can result in loss. Any betting-related feature must be used only where lawful and by users who meet the applicable age requirements.

What are weekly bonuses? ＋

Weekly bonuses are promotional rewards that may be offered for certain activities, milestones or participation. Bonus conditions, expiry dates and eligibility should always be checked before claiming.

How do quizzes work? ＋

Choose an available quiz, answer the questions and follow the instructions shown on screen. Some quizzes may have time limits, attempt limits or specific reward conditions.

When will my rewards appear? ＋

Reward timing depends on the activity. Some rewards may be recorded immediately while others can require verification. Your dashboard should show the status of completed activities and available rewards.

Can I withdraw my rewards? ＋

Withdrawal options should be connected to the real Proftex backend and clearly explained in the account area. Before launching, make sure your actual minimum withdrawal, processing time, supported payment methods and verification requirements are displayed accurately.

Is Proftex free to join? ＋

This depends on the final Proftex registration model. The registration page should clearly state whether registration is free and disclose any fee before a user creates an account or makes a payment.

Why can't I see a particular activity? ＋

Activities can be limited by availability, campaign capacity, location, account eligibility, age requirements or temporary maintenance. Check your dashboard again later or contact support if you believe something is missing.

What should I do if an activity does not credit my reward? ＋

Keep any relevant activity ID, screenshot or completion information, then contact Proftex support. Do not repeatedly submit the same activity if the rules say duplicate attempts are not allowed.
