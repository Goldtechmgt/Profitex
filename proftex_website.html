Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers before you get started.

What is Proftex?＋

Proftex is an entertainment and digital-activity platform concept where users can explore games, music, chat activities, quizzes, promotional spins, weekly bonuses and other available features.

How do I get started?＋

Register through the official Proftex registration page, complete the required account information and open your dashboard to see which activities are available to your account.

What are the ways to earn?＋

The current Proftex concept includes playing games, listening to music, approved chat activities, Spin & Win, prediction content, weekly bonuses and quizzes. More activities can be added later.

Do I have to complete every activity?＋

No. You can choose the activities you are interested in, subject to availability, eligibility and the rules shown for each activity.

Can I use Proftex on my phone?＋

Yes. The website is designed with a responsive layout so users can access the platform from phones, tablets and computers.

How do the game rewards work?＋

Select an eligible game and follow the activity instructions. Any reward should only be credited after the required conditions have been completed and verified where necessary.

How does listening to music work?＋

When a music campaign is available, choose the eligible listening task and follow its requirements. The campaign should explain the applicable reward and any limits before participation.

What are chat activities?＋

Chat activities allow users to participate in approved conversations. Participants should respect one another, follow community rules and avoid sharing passwords, financial information or other sensitive personal details.

How does Spin & Win work?＋

Eligible promotional spins may provide different rewards. The actual reward, limits and eligibility conditions should be shown clearly before a user spins.

Are bet predictions guaranteed?＋

No. Predictions are never guaranteed. Betting can result in financial loss and must only be offered and used where lawful and by users who meet applicable age requirements.

What are weekly bonuses?＋

Weekly bonuses are promotional rewards that can be linked to activity, milestones or special campaigns. Always check the exact conditions, expiry and eligibility before claiming.

How do quizzes work?＋

Choose an available quiz, answer the questions and follow the instructions. Some quizzes can have time limits, attempt limits or specific reward requirements.

When are rewards credited?＋

Timing depends on the activity. Some rewards may appear quickly while others may require verification. Your real dashboard should show the current status of eligible rewards.

How do withdrawals work?＋

Connect this section to your actual Proftex withdrawal system before launch. Clearly display the minimum withdrawal, supported payment methods, processing time and any verification requirements.

Why can't I see an activity?＋

Activities can depend on campaign availability, account eligibility, location, age restrictions, capacity or maintenance. Check the dashboard again later if an activity is temporarily unavailable.

What if an activity doesn't credit my reward?＋

Save the activity details and any relevant screenshot or transaction reference, then contact Proftex support. Avoid duplicate submissions if the activity rules prohibit them.

How do I contact Proftex support?＋

Use the official Proftex WhatsApp support button or the support channel connected to your account. Replace the placeholder WhatsApp link in this file with your real business number before publishing.

Is Proftex free to join?＋

Set this according to your real business model. If registration is free, state that clearly. If there are any fees, disclose the amount and conditions before a user pays.
